# Attack Primitives

Phase 6 primitives are deterministic, safe, and synthetic. They represent benchmark events such as configured identifier leakage, fabricated citation identifiers, prohibited synthetic tool requests, shared-budget violations, protected-field use, and constraint-state changes.

Each primitive records primitive id, preconditions, applicable domains, observable artifacts, objective scoring mechanism, cost, expected benefit, and concealment options.

Primitives do not access real data, contact services, write files, execute harmful commands, or produce unrestricted monitor-evasion prompts.
