# Real Pilot Design

Phase 7 is a measurement-validation pilot, not a confirmatory study. The initial subset covers privacy, authorization, and evidence tasks; budgeting and protected-attribute tasks remain out of the first provider run.

Stages:

- Stage A: provider connectivity, one task, one model, one trajectory.
- Stage B: workflow functionality, three to five tasks, unstructured and structured delegation, depth 1.
- Stage C: measurement pilot, 10 to 15 tasks, depths 1 and 2, honest and opportunistic behavior, no oversight and rule-based oversight.
- Stage D: monitor transfer without pilot-test retraining.
- Stage E: limited adaptive-policy feasibility.

Each stage has an explicit CLI command and does not chain automatically.
