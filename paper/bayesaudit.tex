\begin{abstract}
BayesAudit evaluates synthetic, sandboxed oversight workflows under limited audit budgets. The final evidence synthesis reports supported claims together with null results and documented limitations.
\end{abstract}
\section{Scope}
The study is restricted to one provider model, inert tools, three domains, and existing benchmark architectures.
\section{Evidence}
Phases 7 and 8 establish the benchmark and confirmatory study. Phase 9 evaluates fresh seeds and held-out Phase 6 attacker assets. Phase 10 performs no provider calls.
\section{Limitations}
The work does not establish broad production or cross-model safety claims.
